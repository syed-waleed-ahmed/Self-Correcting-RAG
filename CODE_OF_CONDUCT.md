# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version
2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its
code of conduct. It applies to everyone taking part in the project's spaces:
issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions.

In short:
- be respectful and assume good faith;
- critique ideas and code, not people;
- welcome newcomers and questions at every level;
- harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through
the contact options on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/syed-waleed-ahmed).
Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject
contributions, or block participants, who don't follow this code. The
Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe the steps.

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
